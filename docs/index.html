How a bad record travels

The trap is identical whether the root was capture arithmetic or a blind cutover rule: downstream models trust the record, the floor has no channel back, and the correction arrives weeks late from finance.

    What changes the result

    1. Validate at the point of capture, not in an audit months later.
    2. An executive's override carries that executive's signature.
    3. Make the correct path the faster one.

    The first two need coercive power, which inside a company sits with the board. The third does not: it is a software problem, and the one I build for.

    Level 0: the forensic audit

    Level 0 runs on files the client already has: exports of stock on hand, order lines and receipts, and a physical count when one exists.

    No network access, no installation, no credentials, no floor time, no meeting with IT security, and nothing goes back.

    What it refuses to do matters as much as what it computes.

    • A count sheet without unit costs does not report a zero-dollar impact. An unmeasurable figure is reported as unmeasured, with the reason, and a report that states a number it had no basis for fails verification.

    • An order line missing a field is left out of that measure's denominator, and how many were left out is published beside it, so a shrinking denominator stays visible. A lower-bound estimate is labelled as a lower bound.

    • Each friction pattern needs its own minimum of independent evidence before it fires, and even the strongest finding is reported as a question for a person to confirm.

    It is also the go or no-go for Level 1: if the record cannot be reconciled, nothing should be written on top of it.

    Part III · Level 1

    Level 1: a safe-staging layer outside the accounting core

    Level 1 is where software proposes changes to a client's system of record: a reorder point, a price, a purchase order. Whether from a rule, an optimizer, or an AI agent, it never writes directly.

    1. 1Stage
    2. 2Classify
    3. 3Approve
    4. 4Apply once
    5. 5Audit

    Nothing irreversible reaches the system of record without a named person's approval; everything else keeps a path back.

    AI belongs in interpretation and drafting; determinism belongs in state, arithmetic and writes. When an LLM writes directly to the ledger, you have not deployed an agent. You have deployed an uncontrolled mutation engine inside your accounting core.

    Part IV · Limits

    • I chose them because they went wrong, which is exactly how selection bias works. They illustrate a mechanism; they do not measure how often it happens.

    • Thirty-seven stores, one chain, and a study that does not discuss ERP. Its value is the structure of the error, not the headline number.

    • My reading: for forty years each wave, from ISO 9000 to ERP, SaaS and AI, has promised to tame the floor with a better abstraction. What matters is not the average outcome but the tail.

    • In Chile, electronic invoicing was introduced in 2003 and made mandatory in stages between 2014 and 2018; each document is validated by the tax authority when it is issued, and one that fails validation does not exist as a tax document. Inside a company, the only equivalent of that authority is the board or a regulator. A software vendor cannot take its place; when it tries, it produces the illusion of control.

    • Fix capacity first. New software adds load before it removes any.

    • Without a physical count, dated order lines and a cost column, there is no baseline. Results stay in units; dollar figures would be invented.

    • If a bad record's cost lands in a generic account with no name on it, nobody will defend the correct path when it is slower. Name the owner first.

    • An optimizer fed a wrong record produces a precise wrong answer, faster.
